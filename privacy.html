My Cottage

Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026.

My Cottage is a personal app that runs on a household's own computer or home screen. This page explains what it does with your Google information. The short version: it uses it only to show and manage your own calendar, it keeps it on your own devices, and it never shares or sells it.

What My Cottage can access

  • Google Calendar (read): your events, to show your day and point out things like two plans at the same time.
  • Google Calendar (events): to add, change or remove an event or reminder, and only after you have approved a preview of the change.
  • Gmail (send only): to send a morning summary of your day from your account to your own address. My Cottage cannot read, search or delete any email. Only where the morning summary is used; a household's own hub does not ask for this.

Connecting from a phone

On a household's own hub, Google Calendar is connected from a phone. After you sign in, Google sends your phone's browser to a page on this site (connected.html), which immediately passes it back to your own hub. That page keeps nothing and shares nothing. The one-time code passed along cannot be used without a secret that only your hub holds.

Where your information is kept

Your Google sign-in and anything My Cottage saves (such as labels you give your routines, or which alerts you have dismissed) are stored only on your household's own devices. Calendar events are fetched from Google when they are shown and are not collected anywhere else. The developer of My Cottage does not run a server that receives your data.

Sharing

My Cottage does not sell, share or transfer your Google information to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. If the developer helps look after a household's device remotely, it is only with that household's permission, to keep it working.

Google's rules

My Cottage's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can take away My Cottage's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing the app from your device also removes everything it stored there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
