My Cottage

A private calendar and home app for one household.

My Cottage is a small personal app. It runs on a computer in the user's own home (a PC or a small screen somewhere in the house) and shows the household's day: their Google Calendar, reminders, recipes, a shopping list and a few other everyday things.

It is not a service anyone signs up for. There are no accounts, no ads and no subscriptions. Each household's copy runs on its own devices, and its information stays there.

Why it asks for Google access

  • See your calendars, so it can show your day.
  • Add and change events, but only after you have read a plain-English preview and pressed Save.
  • Send email as you, only to send you your own morning summary of the day. It cannot read your email.
